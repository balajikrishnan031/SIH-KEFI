Victims and witnesses of atrocities under the Scheduled Castes and Scheduled Tribes (Prevention of Atrocities) Act, 1989 frequently suffer from prolonged psychological trauma and severe mental distress long after filing a complaint. Existing mechanisms focus almost exclusively on legal and financial compensation, leaving a critical gap in continuous mental health monitoring and early distress intervention.

PROPOSED SOLUTION (KEFI AI):
KEFI AI (Kinematic Emotion & Forecasting Intervention System) is an enterprise-grade AI continuous monitoring and distress prediction platform developed for the Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment (MoSJE). Named after the Greek origin Kéfi (Κέφι) — representing inner resilience, emotional vitality, and healing after trauma — KEFI proactively monitors crime victims throughout the legal, investigation, court trial, compensation, and rehabilitation journey.

1. Multichannel Continuous Interaction Hub: Proactively conducts periodic wellness check-ins via IVRS 14566, AI Chatbot, SMS, Mobile App, and Web Portal using digital OTP consent and end-to-end AES-256 encryption.
2. Dual-Risk Assessment Engine: Separates immediate physical safety threats (intimidation/coercion language) from psychological distress.
3. Mathematical Dynamic Distress Score (DDS): Calculates a continuous multi-signal distress score:
   DDS = 0.25*E + 0.20*V + 0.15*B + 0.15*H + 0.10*C + 0.15*T
   Where E=Emotion/Sentiment, V=Voice Acoustic Prosody, B=Behavioral Check-in Gaps, H=Historical Trend Velocity, C=Case Stage Pressure, T=Threat Keyword Density.
4. Longitudinal Trend & 72-Hour Crisis Forecasting: Calculates trend velocity (dDDS/dt) to predict psychological crises 72 hours before escalation occurs.
5. Explainable AI (SHAP / LIME XAI): Delivers transparent point attributions (+21 threat, +18 fear, +14 velocity, +12 missed check-ins, +9 voice stress) for audit-ready officer decisions.
6. 3-Tier Action Matrix:
   - Green/Yellow (0-30): Routine wellness check-ins & self-help grounding tools.
   - Orange (31-60): Focused check-ins & scheduled tele-counselling.
   - Red (61-100): Real-time alert to District Magistrate (DM) / SP dashboard, witness protection & crisis team dispatch.

BENEFICIARY IMPACT:
Protects SC/ST atrocity victims, rape/gang rape survivors, families of caste-based violence, and witnesses facing court trial intimidation. Rebuilds victim confidence in the justice delivery system and enables evidence-based decision making for MoSJE administrators. Fully DPDP Act 2023 compliant.

1. BACKGROUND AND DETAILED PROBLEM STATEMENT
Victims of atrocities frequently experience prolonged, unaddressed psychological distress after registering a complaint. Traumatic stress escalates over months due to:
- Intimidation and threats from accused persons demanding case withdrawal.
- Repeated court appearances and cross-examinations.
- Delays in police investigation and judicial trials.
- Social ostracism and isolation within local communities.
- Economic hardship and loss of livelihood.
Existing statutory mechanisms under the SC/ST (Prevention of Atrocities) Act 1989 focus on post-hoc monetary compensation and legal procedures, but lack any system for continuous mental health tracking or early distress crisis prediction.

2. KEFI AI SYSTEM ARCHITECTURE & CORE INNOVATION
KEFI AI (Kinematic Emotion & Forecasting Intervention System) is built specifically for the Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment (MoSJE) to provide continuous, proactive mental health monitoring and automated intervention dispatch.

A. MULTICHANNEL INGESTION & CONSENT
- Registration via NHAA (14566), Integrated Portal, Mobile App, IVRS, or Web Portal.
- Secure onboarding with OTP authentication, DPDP Act 2023 digital consent, and AES-256 encryption.

B. MULTIMODAL AI ANALYSIS ENGINE
- NLP & Sentiment AI: Fine-tuned Transformer models evaluating text messages for emotional valence, hopelessness, and threat keywords ('dhamki', 'withdraw', 'court').
- Voice Acoustic Prosody Analyzer: Processes voice call recordings to extract pitch tremor (Hz), vocal jitter %, pause duration, and speech stress index (0.0 to 1.0).
- Behavioral Engagement Engine: Measures missed periodic check-in frequency and baseline engagement drop.
- Field Welfare Officer Inputs: Ground-level observation notes logged by visiting social workers.

C. DYNAMIC DISTRESS SCORE (DDS) & FORECASTING MODEL
Mathematical Formulation:
DDS = 0.25*E + 0.20*V + 0.15*B + 0.15*H + 0.10*C + 0.15*T
Where:
- E (0.25): Emotion & Sentiment Score
- V (0.20): Voice Stress Index
- B (0.15): Behavioral Check-in Gap Rate
- H (0.15): Historical Trend Velocity (dDDS/dt)
- C (0.10): Court Hearing Proximity Factor
- T (0.15): Intimidation Keyword Density

Longitudinal Forecasting: Predicts distress trajectories for 24h, 48h, and 72h using time-series trend velocity modeling to intercept mental crises before escalation.

D. EXPLAINABLE AI (XAI) & ACTION MATRIX
SHAP and LIME hybrid point attributions generate audit-ready explanation cards (+21 threat language, +18 fear sentiment, +14 trend velocity, +12 missed check-ins, +9 voice jitter) for District Magistrates and Superintendents of Police.

Risk Band Classification:
- Green / Yellow (0-30): Routine support, self-help grounding audio, box breathing, SC/ST PoA Act rights pamphlets.
- Orange (31-60): Focused check-ins, scheduled tele-counselling & legal guidance.
- Red (61-100): Immediate alert generated on DM / SP real-time dashboard, witness protection & crisis team dispatch.

3. FEASIBILITY, PRIVACY & LEGAL COMPLIANCE
- Seamless integration with NHAA (14566) helpline infrastructure.
- Low Internet / Offline Mode for remote rural areas with basic SMS and pre-cached grounding tools.
- Multilingual support including Tanglish, Tamil, Hindi, and English.
- Silent Mode & Posture/Face Affect detection for situations where victims cannot speak or type.
- Full compliance with DPDP Act 2023, data minimization, explicit consent, and victim data purge controls.

4. EXPECTED OUTCOMES & IMPACT
- Early detection and prevention of mental health crises among SC/ST atrocity victims.
- Reduced witness retraction and trial dropouts due to continuous state protection.
- Evidence-based decision making for MoSJE administrators and District Magistrates.
